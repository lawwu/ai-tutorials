# resources/ — the logistics knowledge base

Everything about your charter, umbrella school, co-op, vendors, testing,
library, and field trips. One page per topic.

**Start here for any question about deadlines, funds, or paperwork.** Keep
this README as an index — one line per page — so the assistant can find
things without reading every file.

## What belongs here

- Charter or umbrella school: reporting periods, work-sample requirements,
  attendance rules, who to email about what
- Vendors: what they cost, who funds them, which day, their own calendar
- Testing: what's required, when, what the results mean
- Library: card numbers *(no PINs)*, hold policies, branch hours
- Field trips and community days

## What does NOT belong here

Anything on the "never commit" list in `CLAUDE.md` — addresses, identity
documents, student ID numbers, medical information, passwords, account
numbers. Those live in your charter's own portal, not in this folder.

A useful test: if this folder leaked, would this line embarrass or endanger
anyone? Vendor calendars, no. A student identifier, yes.

## Why it's worth the effort

The deadline you'll miss is the one nobody wrote down. When a page here
records "purchase orders due the 15th, submitted through the parent portal,
approved in about a week," the assistant can put it on your week brief
before it's urgent — and when it tells you something is due, you can ask
"how do you know that?" and get a file to check.
