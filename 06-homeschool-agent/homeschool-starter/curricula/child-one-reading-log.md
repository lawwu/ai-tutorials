that are already here.

This log feeds two things you'll want later: engagement minutes for
record-keeping, and choosing work samples at reporting time.

aloud with a parent), or `audio`.

## Their own summaries

When a child writes or dictates their own summary of a book, keep it here
**verbatim** — spelling, grammar, and all. Cleaning it up destroys the
record of how their writing is actually developing, which is the thing you'd
most want to look back at in a year.
