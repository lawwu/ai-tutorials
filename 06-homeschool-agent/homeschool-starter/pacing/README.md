# pacing/ — templates and reports

Two different things live here.

## Weekly templates

`<child>-weekly-template.md` — the shape of a normal week for that child,
per weekday. The daily pages in `plans/` get printed from these, so a change
here changes every future week.

A template is a rhythm, not a schedule. It says "Monday: math, history,
independent reading," not "9:03 math." Time-of-day belongs on the daily page
if it belongs anywhere.

## Reports

`reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md` — the output of a pacing check, kept so you can see
the trend over the year rather than just today's verdict.

## What a pacing report should say

For each child, four things and then a plain verdict:

- Current position and percent complete
- Lessons per week required to finish by the target date
- Actual observed pace, from the journal
- **On track / speed up / slow down**

"Behind pace" is an acceptable answer when mastery is the reason. The report
should flag it without panic — the point of tracking pace is to notice
drift early, not to push a child through material they don't have yet.

Ask for one:

```
pacing report — are we on track to finish math by June?
```
